# Data from: Effects of different irrigation lower limits and combined application of reduced nitrogen with organic fertilizer on spring-season tomatoes (*Solanum lycopersicum*)

Dataset DOI: [10.5061/dryad.wm37pvn33](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.wm37pvn33)

## Description of the data and file structure

### Files and variables

#### File: 2024.06.05_Quality_Determination.xlsx

**Description:** The measurement data of soluble solids, vitamin C (VC), soluble acids, and soluble sugars in tomatoes, as well as the calculated sugar-acid ratio data.

##### Variables

* Code:The identifier for each sample, corresponding to 12 experimental treatments (each treatment includes 3 replicates), which is used to trace the source of the sample and match it with the treatment design.
* Average value (%)：The average of the sample data.
* Numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6:the sample identification labels.
* Soluble Solids:Total dissolved substances in juice, measured in °Brix via refractometry.
* VC (Vitamin C):Ascorbic acid, a key nutritional and antioxidant component determined by titration or spectrophotometry.
* Soluble Acids:Titratable organic acids in juice, measured by neutralization titration with NaOH.
* Soluble Sugars:Low-molecular-weight carbohydrates (glucose, fructose, sucrose) quantified via spectrophotometry or HPLC.
* Sugar-Acid Ratio:Ratio of soluble sugar content to titratable acid content, an indicator of flavor balance.

#### File: Irrigation_Data_1-4.xlsx

**Description:** The irrigation records from the four irrigation treatment plots.

##### Variables

* Date:Irrigation dates of the irrigation treatment plots
* Irrigation Status:On-off status of irrigation volume monitoring equipment
* Daily Irrigation Volume (m³):The total amount of water irrigated to crops per day.The unit is cubic meters (m³), which records the daily water supply of each irrigation treatment plot.
* Water Meter Reading (m³): The cumulative water consumption value recorded by a water meter. The unit is cubic meters (m³). It is used to record and calculate the actual irrigation water amount of each experimental treatment plot.
* Cumulative Water Consumption of Site (m³): The total accumulated water consumption of the experimental site over a certain period. It is calculated by summing up the water usage each time, with the unit being cubic meters (m³).

#### File: Photosynthetic_Data.xlsx

Description:Photosynthetic measurement data of five samples per treatment in this experimen&#x74;**,** with twelve treatments in total.

##### Variables

* Code:The identifier for each sample, corresponding to 12 experimental treatments (each treatment includes 5 replicates), which is used to trace the source of the sample and match it with the treatment design.
* Transpiration rate：The rate of water vapor loss from the leaf surface per unit leaf area per unit time, reflecting the leaf water transpiration capacity.
* Net photosynthetic rate：The net rate of carbon fixation in leaves, calculated as gross photosynthesis minus respiratory consumption; the core index reflecting photosynthetic productivity.
* Intercellular CO₂ concentration：CO₂concentration in the intercellular air space of leaves, an important parameter for judging stomatal and non-stomatal limitation of photosynthesis.
* Stomatal conductance：A measure of the degree of stomatal opening, reflecting the ability of stomata to conduct CO₂and water vapor, which directly affects photosynthesis and transpiration.

#### File: Statistics_Table_of_Leaf__Stem_and_Fruit_Weight.xlsx

**Description:** Statistics Table of Leaf, Stem, and Fruit Weight.xlsx: Contains the raw measurement data of plant organ weights to support subsequent statistical analyses (i.e., leaf weight, stem weight, fruit weight). All data values are actual measured results without NA values, ensuring data integrity for analysis.

##### Variables

* Code:The identifier for each sample, corresponding to 12 experimental treatments (The leaf and stem treatment included 3 samples, and the fruit treatment included 5 samples.), which is used to trace the source of the sample and match it with the treatment design.
* Leaf(g)、Stem(g)、Fresh fruit weight(g)：It represents the weight of leaves, stems, and fruits, with the unit being grams.
* Nitrogen proportion(%)、Phosphorus proportion(%)、Potassium proportion(%)：Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium Proportions of Leaf, Stem and Fruit Samples
* Nitrogen content(g)、Phosphorus content(g)、Potassium content(g)：Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Potassium Contents in Different Parts of Each Sample
